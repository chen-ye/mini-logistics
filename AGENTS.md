# AGENTS.md — Development & Review Methodology

## 1. Project Vision & North Star

**Mini Logistics** is a 2D procedural world generator and multi-modal transportation logistics simulator patterned after **OpenTTD** (_TerraGenesis_) and **Mini Motorways** / **Mini Metro**.

Every change to the generator, renderer, or simulation must balance two complementary forces:

1. **Cartographic Minimalism (_Mini Motorways_ / _Mini Metro_)**: Warm paper palettes, octilinear Marching-Squares contours, bold readable stroke weights, clear visual hierarchy, and uncluttered negative space.
2. **Geographic & Engineering Verisimilitude (_OpenTTD_)**: Landforms, watersheds, settlement hierarchies, RCI urban blocks, airport approach funnels, grade-separated expressways, interchanges, and industrial supply chains that behave according to real-world physical geography and civil/transport engineering.

## 2. Iterative Visual Development & Dual-Subagent Review Methodology

Never develop or tune procedural generation rules blind, and never approach visual problems purely algorithmically without first inspecting and comparing rendered worlds across waves.

### Step 1 — First-Principles Visual Comparison Before Coding

- When refining terrain, hydrology, urban morphology, or highway layout, start by visually comparing current screenshots against earlier waves and against the aesthetic baseline (_Mini Motorways_ and _OpenTTD_).
- Diagnose why an artifact looks unnatural to a human viewer (e.g., waffle-iron grid regularity, reverse-tapering rivers that read backwards, or camera margins that make border rivers appear to stop in mid-land) before changing parameters.

### Step 2 — Real Browser Verification Across Seeds, Archetypes & Engines

- Serve the app locally (`http://127.0.0.1:8788/index.html`) and capture full-viewport (`1600×1000`) and mobile (`390×844`) screenshots in a real headless browser across multiple benchmark seeds and archetypes:
  - Benchmark seeds across the 9 orthogonal landform archetypes (`confluence`, `inland`, `alpine-passes`, `fjord-sound`, `isthmus-strait`, `caldera-basin`, `barrier-lagoon`, `canyon-gorge`, `island-hopper`), the 6 continuous geomorphology scalars in [ARCHETYPE_PARAMETER_PRESETS](./src/constants.js#L207-L289), the 3 [Stage 2A Terrain Engines](./src/terrain-solvers.js#L105-L923), and the 3 [Stage 2B Hydrology Engines](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L24-L1201).
  - Multi-island seeds (`ISLAND-HOP-101`, `ARCHIPELAGO-77`, `AEGEAN-420`, `ATOLL-808`) in the `island-hopper` scenario.
  - Continental benchmark seeds (`OTTD-METRO-84`, `DELTA-402`, `HARBOR-305`, `VALLEY-618`).
  - Both the **Mini Motorways View** (`aesthetic`), the **RCI & Cargo Chain View** (`rci`), and the **Road Grid & Graph View** (`roadgrid`).
- When modifying code that runs inside the [world Web Worker](./src/world-worker.js#L1-L29) or [VectorLayerRecorder display-list cache](./src/canvas-layer-cache.js#L144-L759), always reload the page in the browser before capturing screenshots so the Worker thread and main-thread modules reload updated ES modules.
- Run the automated static and behavioral verification suite (`bun run check`: `bun fmt --no-script --check`, `bunx oxlint --deny-warnings`, `bun check --no-script`, `bun test --check tests/unit`, and `bunx playwright test`) before concluding any round.

### Step 3 — Parallel Dual-Subagent Review Loop (Targeted + Uncued Holistic Reviewer)

Every major iteration must be audited by **two independent review subagents dispatched in parallel**:

1. **Targeted Round Reviewer**:
   - Verifies the specific invariants, bug fixes, and geometric constraints targeted in the current round.
2. **Uncued Holistic Rubric Reviewer (Mandatory in Parallel)**:
   - Spawned in a separate session **without** being over-cued on the specific round's code changes or implementation details, so its eyes remain completely unbiased.
   - Evaluates the generated world screenshots purely as a player, cartographer, and game designer against the **4-Pillar Success Rubric**:
     - **Aesthetic Quality**: Visual harmony, stroke weight hierarchy, contour cleanliness (no plaid grid cross-hatching), cohesive woodland canopies, and _Mini Motorways_-grade polish.
     - **Verisimilitude**: Stylized abstraction in art direction and scale that remains grounded in real physical geography, watershed hydrology, urban land-use patterns, and highway/airport engineering.
     - **Plausibility & Anti-Absurdity**: Hunts specifically for anything that looks absurd or broken to a human observer—such as road networks that _almost_ connect across a tiny gap, local roads wrapping in tight U-loops around river spring tips, expressways running longitudinally inside rivers or making sharp un-ramped V-turns, surface interchanges built on top of underground tunnels, rivers bouncing off map borders or splitting downstream, or awkward building/airport placements.
     - **Gameplay & Directional Fit (_OpenTTD_ × _Mini Motorways_)**: Evaluates whether the world reads as an inviting, legible, strategic transportation and cargo puzzle with clear geographic bottlenecks, distinct regional hubs, and readable RCI supply-chain flows.

### Step 4 — Commit Each Verified Round

- Commit each verified round to git with a clear message before starting the next wave so visual diffs and historical screenshots can be compared side-by-side.

## 3. Core Architectural & Domain Invariants

### Pluggable Hourglass Architecture

- Keep [Stage 2A Terrain Synthesis](./src/terrain-solvers.js#L105-L923), [Stage 2B Fluvial Hydrology](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L24-L1201), and [Stages 3–9 Urban & Transport Generation](./src/world-generator.js#L501-L2213) decoupled across the narrow `(elevationField, seaLevel, waterMask, rivers)` data contract in [src/types.d.ts](./src/types.d.ts#L1-L1020) and [src/constants.js](./src/constants.js#L1-L311) so all $3 \times 3$ Terrain $\times$ Hydrology combinations remain hot-swappable in-game.
- Keep world generation off the main thread in the [world module Web Worker](./src/world-worker.js#L1-L29) using [zero-copy Transferable ArrayBuffers](./src/world-generator.js#L2219-L2295), and record static terrain, hydrology, urban, road, building, airport, motorway, and airway layers into the [retained `VectorLayerRecorder` `Path2D` display lists](./src/renderer.js#L175-L450) (`hydroDisplayList`, `urbanTerrainDisplayList`, `surfaceNetworkDisplayList`, `motorwayDisplayList`, `overpassBridgeDisplayList`, and `airwayDisplayList`) in [src/canvas-layer-cache.js](./src/canvas-layer-cache.js#L144-L759).

### Hydrology & Landform Invariants

- **Nine Orthogonal Landform Archetypes & Continuous 6-Scalar Geomorphology Basis**: Stage 2A evaluates a continuous scalar basis parameterized by `(terrainRelief, valleyIncision, coastalEmbayment, calderaCurvature, barrierFragmentation, basinCount)` in [ARCHETYPE_PARAMETER_PRESETS](./src/constants.js#L207-L289) and [synthesizeTerrainAndBasins](./src/terrain-solvers.js#L105-L923). Border suppression on `inland` and `coastalEmbayment === 0` strictly clamps border tiles (`x <= 3 || x >= W - 4 || y <= 3 || y >= H - 4`) to `seaLevel + 0.04` and clears `waterMask`, strictly preserving 100% landlocked topography with zero border-touching sea basins. `canyon-gorge` enforces plateau terrace step-flattening and canyon cliffs; `caldera-basin` models radial Somma rings, crater depressions, and resurgent domes; and `island-hopper` drives volcanic island arc curvature and multi-lobe archipelago massif separation.
- **Bold Cartographic River Proportions**: Mainstem rivers scale from `1.85 → 3.20` tiles wide and carve `waterMask = 1` across their full physical half-width (`chanRad >= 1.05`) in [carveTaperingRiverPath](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L660-L890) so waterfront setbacks and bank-to-bank bridges align with the rendered water ribbon.
- **Smoothed-Octilinear Rivers & Shorelines**: Mainland rivers route along multi-tile octilinear runs (0°/45°/90°) between 4.2-tile sampled waypoints, scoring distance and directional alignment to eliminate 1-tile H-V-H-V staircase chatter while preserving 8-connected continuity in [smoothPolyline](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L62-L295). Coastlines and lake shorelines in [buildVignelliWaterPath](./src/canvas-layer-cache.js#L778-L1120) chain Marching-Squares segments into continuous closed loops, collapsing 1-cell staircase teeth and applying soft Bezier corner fillets. River channels extrude into closed clockwise-wound polygons with quadratic Bezier corner fillets in [extrudeRibbonToPath2D](./src/canvas-layer-cache.js#L1128-L1260) and [drawTaperingRivers](./src/canvas-layer-cache.js#L1276-L1410).
- **No Mid-Land Dead-End Tentacles or Border Bounce-Backs**: Mainland trunk rivers originate at [map-border valley entries](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L577-L658) (clear of floating HUD corners), upstream lake outlets, or [wooded interior highland springs](./src/hydrology-solvers.js#L521-L575) (`0.55 → 1.45` tiles wide, nestled inside forest groves with a 6-tile road exclusion buffer around `headwaterPt`). Any interior river approaching within 22 tiles of a map border snaps cleanly off-canvas.

### Urban, Industrial & Trade Gateway Invariants

- **5-Phase Demand-Driven Pull Supply Chain & 100% Connectivity Guarantee**: [buildIndustrialCargoChain](./src/vehicle-routing.js#L1696-L2620) coordinates the regional supply chain in 5 stages:
  - _Step 0_: Normalizes surviving post-highway demolition lots and synthesizes virtual gateway proxy destinations (`id = 10000 + entityId`) from [TradeGateway](./src/types.d.ts#L129-L150) nodes.
  - _Step 1_: Commercial buildings pull matching goods from regional `LOG` hubs (`LOG->COM`, `#7c3aed`), direct `MFG` factories (`MFG->COM`, `#4f46e5`), or gateway imports (`IMPORT->COM`, `#0d9488`), assigning distinct `demandedGoods` without replacement per town first, consolidating ~50–65% of retail freight through regional `LOG` hubs, and aggregating demand profiles onto `LOG` hubs.
  - _Step 2_: Direct inter-CBD wholesale shipping (`CBD->CBD`, `#0284c7`, `B2B_PARCELS`) links central downtown CBD high-rises across town pairs.
  - _Step 3_: `LOG` hubs pull inventory proportional to `relativeDemand` (`2..6` inbound links) from matching `MFG` factories (`MFG->LOG`, `#ea580c`) or gateway proxies (`IMPORT->LOG`, `#0d9488`).
  - _Step 4_: Factories pull required 2-input feedstock (`STEEL` pulls `ORE`+`WOOD`; `FOOD` pulls `AGRI`+`CHEM`; `GOODS` pulls `WOOD`+`HARDWARE`; `TECH` pulls `CHEM`+`HARDWARE`) using a regional load-balanced scoring pass, falling back to gateway imports (`IMPORT->MFG`) to guarantee 100% of factories receive input.
  - _Step 5_: Trade gateways pull export links (`RAW->EXPORT`, `MFG->EXPORT`, `LOG->EXPORT`), followed by a final topological guarantee ensuring every reachable raw extractor has $\ge 1$ outbound link, every factory and log hub has $\ge 1$ inbound and $\ge 1$ outbound link, and every commercial building has $\ge 1$ inbound link.
- **5 `RAW` Extraction Niches, 4 Bespoke `MFG` Silhouettes & RCI Visual Polish**: Rural resource siting in [seedOutOfTownResourceNiches](./src/urban-fabric-solver.js#L1345-L1715) seeds 5 distinct `RAW` niches: `MINE` ($\rightarrow$ `ORE`), `TIMBER` ($\rightarrow$ `WOOD`), `FARM` ($\rightarrow$ `AGRI`), coastal shoreline wharf `FISHERY` ($\rightarrow$ `AGRI`, sited on a dry `2×2` pad + 1-tile setback with `dW in [1.1, 2.8]`, deep marine cyan `#0e7490`), and peri-urban `CHEM` ($\rightarrow$ `CHEM`, olive-lime `#4d7c0f`). In-town industrial zoning in [placeTownCommercialAndIndustrial](./src/urban-fabric-solver.js#L2075-L2455) places 4 specialized `MFG` archetypes (`STEEL`, `FOOD`, `GOODS`, `TECH`) rendered with bespoke architectural roof silhouettes in [drawBuildings](./src/renderer.js#L2109-L2325), `LOG` distribution centers with relative-demand shares, and commercial lots with distinct [demandedGoods](./src/types.d.ts#L384). RCI badges in [drawRCICargoChainOverlay](./src/renderer.js#L4310-L4495) anchor above each building's `hTiles` footprint and suppress base pills on selected/partner buildings in [drawSelectionHighlight](./src/renderer.js#L5008-L5225), while [<ml-entity-inspector>](./src/inspector-component.js#L53-L995) displays stage-colored partner link badges and explicit monospace commodity chips.
- **Multi-Modal Trade Gateways & External Corridors**: Placed airports initialize [TradeGateway](./src/types.d.ts#L129-L150) nodes with outward boundary-projected [ExternalCorridor](./src/types.d.ts#L118-L127) vectors (`HUB`/`CRG` dual corridors vs `REG` single corridor) in [solveAndPlaceAirports](./src/airport-solver.js#L84-L600). Virtual gateway proxy destinations (`id = 10000 + entityId`) integrate into road vehicle routing for multimodal freight transfer.
- **Organic Unphased Boroughs & Multi-Size RCI Lots**: Boroughs use independent grid phases with a 2-tile parallel-street inhibition guard (forming natural T-junctions), variable `3/4/5`-tile block strides, and multi-dimension footprints (`3×2`, `2×3`, `2×2`, `2×1`, `1×2`) with yard breathing room and outer empty-street pruning in [growUrbanFabric](./src/urban-fabric-solver.js#L1313-L2445) and [buildFarmEstate](./src/farm-estate-solver.js#L10-L1260).

### Road, Expressway & Aviation Trajectory Invariants

- **Road & Expressway Trajectory Guards**:
  - Roads must cross rivers from bank to bank within 5 steps in [findRoadPathAStar](./src/urban-fabric-solver.js#L547-L895) and never braid lengthwise along river channels, with [deriveRoadTopology](./src/vehicle-routing.js#L149-L155) and [ensureVehicleRoutingGraph](./src/vehicle-routing.js#L395-L954) feeding [MiniMotorwaysRenderer](./src/renderer.js#L46-L4953) directly downstream. Vehicle trajectories must stay strictly on committed pavement (`<= 0.55` tiles) across topological roundabout portals and directed Cloverleaf/Trumpet Bezier ramp chains in [buildDirectedLaneTrajectory](./src/vehicle-routing.js#L1382-L1600).
  - Expressways must never cross airport pads, must never run longitudinally inside rivers (`riverProxArc <= 14.5`), must never make acute V-turns (`cosTurn >= 0.35`, `cosWide >= 0.44`), and may only share a town beltway roundabout when departing tangents are $\ge 110^\circ$ apart (`dot <= -0.34`) in [scoreMotorwaySamples](./src/expressway-solver.js#L449-L850). Highway curves resample to uniform arc-length steps (`targetDensity = 1.25`, `steps >= 64`, average step length `<= 0.95` tiles) in [sampleMotorwayCurve](./src/expressway-solver.js#L1370-L1425).
  - Resized Cloverleaf interchanges (`outerDist = 3.65`, `leafSpan = 4.85`), 4-ramp Trumpet spurs (left outer slip-ramp, right outer slip-ramp, and concentric inner 270° bell loop + outer jug-handle bell), and compacted Diamond underpasses (`walked = 2.35`, `stepReach ≈ 2.15`, terminal vertex distance `Math.hypot(posTerm - center) <= 3.6` tiles) enforce strict 1.50-tile house corridor clearance along all 4 cubic Bezier slip-ramps in [clearBuildingsAndTreesAlongDiamondRamps](./src/expressway-solver.js#L2616-L2675).
  - Subterranean mountain tunnels (`elevationField >= seaLevel + 0.235` per [WORLD_GEOMETRY.ELEVATION_THRESHOLDS](./src/constants.js#L48-L53)) must cast zero ground shadows, render full-width dashed bore walls, and never coalesce across a surface gap that contains a Cloverleaf or Trumpet interchange in [detectMountainExpresswayTunnels](./src/expressway-solver.js#L4111-L4190).
  - Overpass Z-Order & 2-Plane Shadows: Motorway ground shadows coalesce into a single unified `groundShadowUnionPath` across crossing ramps and mainlines to prevent double-dark overlap artifacts. Grade-separated overpasses render in a dedicated retained `overpassBridgeDisplayList` above crossing lower carriageways, casting clipped lower-deck drop shadows and carrying upper-deck bridge decks with parapet railings and `onUpperBridge` routing graph node tagging in [ensureVehicleRoutingGraph](./src/vehicle-routing.js#L395-L954) for live elevated vehicle simulation.
- **Three-Class Aviation Fleet, ATC & Trajectory Guards**:
  - Aircraft must follow [into-the-wind AirportATC controllers and 10-state aircraft agent state machines](./src/simulator.js#L100-L2706) (`GATE_LOADING -> PUSHBACK_HOLD -> TAXI_TO_RUNWAY -> HOLD_SHORT_QUEUE -> TAKEOFF_ROLL -> ENROUTE_CRUISE -> APPROACH_QUEUE -> LANDING_ROLLOUT -> HOLD_CROSSING_RUNWAY -> TAXI_TO_GATE`) at invariant world-space speeds (`tiles/sec`), keeping parked gate aircraft in their dedicated apron bay (`|gate.ly - taxiY| >= 1.4` tiles) off-spine while enforcing 1D leader-follower non-passing separation (`>= 2.4` tiles) on `taxiwayOccupants`, separating departures (`mainRwyY = -1.72*ts`) and arrivals (`outerRwyY = -3.35*ts`) on Dual-Runway Hubs, enforcing hold-short locks on single-runway airfields, and rendering ground-taxiing vs. airborne Z-layers with apron gate T-markings, district livery accents, and quad-nacelle air cargo freighters in [MiniMotorwaysRenderer](./src/renderer.js#L3585-L3890).
  - Aircraft agents in [LogisticsSimulator](./src/simulator.js#L963-L2706) operate as a 3-class fleet: `WIDEBODY_FREIGHTER`, `FEEDER_TURBOPROP`, or `PASSENGER_JET`. Enforce strict wide-body eligibility guard [canWidebodyServe](./src/simulator.js#L444-L459): wide-body freighters are restricted to `HUB` and `CRG` airfields or off-map external corridors and must never land at or depart from `REG` spoke airports. Feeder turboprops serve all airports including `REG` at dedicated calibrated flight speeds (`10.6 / 13.2 / 9.8` tiles/sec in [SIM_CONFIG.AVIATION](./src/constants.js#L86-L103)). Off-map external flights in [buildFlightTrack](./src/simulator.js#L517-L930) transition seamlessly across map borders at cruise altitude with automated international turnaround and import/export tracking. Demand-weighted freight truck spawning via [spawnCar(true)](./src/simulator.js#L1017-L1106) scales with `cargoLink.demandWeight`.

### State Architecture & URL Serialization Invariants

- **Store as Single Source of Truth Across 4 Domains**: Keep `seed`, map gen parameters, view state (`activeLayer`, `drawerOpen`, viewport-independent `camera: { cx, cy, zoom } | null`), and interaction state (`simSpeed`, `inspected: { type, id, entity }`) strictly inside the selector store in [src/state.js](./src/state.js#L279-L686) and [src/types.d.ts](./src/types.d.ts#L991-L998), eliminating DOM scraping.
- **Pure Boundary Parsing & Canonical Query Strings**: Parse and validate all search query parameters at the system boundary in [src/url-state.js](./src/url-state.js#L128-L590). Sliders clamp to valid ranges, invalid enum values fall back cleanly to defaults, and `camera` requires complete, finite `(cx, cy, z)` triples (`z` in `[0.16, 3.5]`). Validates all 9 orthogonal landform archetypes (`confluence`, `inland`, `alpine-passes`, `fjord-sound`, `isthmus-strait`, `caldera-basin`, `barrier-lagoon`, `canyon-gorge`, `island-hopper`) and the 6 continuous geomorphology scalars (`relief`, `incision`, `indent`, `caldera`, `barrier`, `basins`).
- **Gesture-End Commit & History Navigation Separation**: Drag-pan and pinch-zoom mutate memory at 60fps and commit to the store and URL strictly upon gesture completion (`pointerup`/`pointercancel`), with a 150ms debounce solely for `wheel` ticks in [src/main.js](./src/main.js#L1347-L1419). Discrete navigational changes (`seed`, `worldSize`, `archetype`, and entity inspector drill-down) commit via `history.pushState`, while continuous adjustments (slider commits on `change`, camera gestures, simulation speed, drawer state) update in place via `history.replaceState` protected by a re-entrant `popstate` guard.
